# FinMate – Student FinTech Product Strategy

FinMate is a student-focused fintech product concept developed for **ProduScope 2025: The Product Case Study Competition, IIT Guwahati**, where the project secured **Rank 1**.

The project combines **primary research, product analytics, feature prioritization, GTM strategy, and AI-assisted prototyping** to address common financial challenges faced by college students.

## Problem Statement

College students increasingly use digital payment and finance apps, but still face challenges around:

- Managing monthly allowances
- Tracking everyday expenses
- Building consistent savings habits
- Handling roommate rent and shared expenses
- Managing irregular side income
- Understanding basic personal finance

FinMate was designed as a student-specific financial management platform addressing these gaps.

## Data Analysis

Primary survey data from **1,000 Indian college students** was analyzed using Python and Pandas.

Key findings:

- **82%** of students already use a finance app
- **51%** have a part-time job
- Average monthly allowance: **₹4,918**
- **41.3%** listed loans among their top financial challenges
- **39.8%** listed saving among their top financial challenges

Behavioral segments were created based on part-time employment and finance-app usage to understand differences in student financial behavior.

The analysis was then used to guide product feature selection and prioritization.

## Product Strategy

Features were prioritized using the **RICE framework** based on Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort.

Core MVP features included:

- Smart Savings Pods
- College Expense Tracker
- Roommate Rent Split
- Side Income Tracker
- Behavioral Nudges
- Gamified Money Missions

Future roadmap features included Campus Score and student-focused credit products.

## Go-To-Market Strategy

A **6-month rollout roadmap** was developed covering:

- Market validation
- Campus selection
- MVP development
- Pilot rollout
- Student ambassador activation
- Engagement and retention
- Expansion and monetization

The GTM strategy focused on campus communities, PG/hostel ecosystems, student ambassadors, and peer-driven adoption.
